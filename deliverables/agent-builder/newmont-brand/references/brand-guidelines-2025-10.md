# Newmont Brand Identity Guidelines, October 2025 edition (reference)

- Source: `Brand Guidelines October 2025.pdf` ("Brand Identity Guidelines, OCTOBER 2025 EDITION"), 47 pages.
- Page numbers below are the PDF's own page numbers.
- Covers: logo, monogram, legal name, co-branding, BRG marks and word marks, the colour palette with tints and a WCAG contrast matrix, topographic patterns and gradients, icons, charts and infographics, photography, typography (Open Sans + Montserrat, Verdana/Tahoma fallbacks), the document grid and percentage layout system, stationery and Word templates (standard document, report/memo, cover page, certificates, fact sheet), email signature, banners and signage, video bumpers, social media, email templates, embroidery/foil, promotional items and PPE.
- Values are copied as printed. Where the PDF contradicts itself or has a typo, the note says so and nothing is corrected silently.
- Hard rule (p46): no external digital or print material may use Newmont branding without prior Corporate Communications approval. Contact: globalcommunications@newmont.com.

## 1. Legal name, boilerplate, co-branding (p3, p6)

**Boilerplate (p3, verbatim):**
> Newmont is the world's leading gold company and a producer of copper, zinc, lead and silver. The Company's world-class portfolio of assets, prospects and talent is anchored in favorable mining jurisdictions in Africa, Australia, Latin America and Caribbean, North America, and Papua New Guinea. Newmont is the only gold producer listed in the S&P 500 Index and is widely recognized for its principled environmental, social and governance practices. Newmont is an industry leader in value creation, supported by robust safety standards, superior execution and technical expertise. Founded in 1921, the Company has been publicly traded since 1925.

The fact sheet (p34) uses an older variant: "the industry's leading gold and copper business and a producer of zinc, lead and silver". Use the p3 text.

**Legal name (p6)**
- Use "Newmont Corporation" in address blocks, legal copy, and formal document titles, headers and footers.
- Inside paragraphs, use "Newmont". "the Company" (capital C) is also acceptable.
- Legal copy: `© Copyright 2025 Newmont Corporation`.
- Address block: Newmont Corporation / 6900 E Layton Avenue / Suite 700 / Denver, CO 80237. Phone T 303.863.7414, F 303.837.5837, newmont.com (p22, p47).

**Co-branding (p6)**
- Logos are equal in size and prominence.
- The partner logo sits 1X from the right edge of the Newmont signature. A 1pt divider line, gray whenever possible, goes between them.
- Horizontal and vertical lockups are both shown.
- In presentations, Newmont's logo comes first: on the left when side by side, or at the top or far left with the partner at the bottom or far right.
- Corporate Communications must approve every co-branded logo.

**Sponsorship (p6)**
- Use the logo only, never the monogram.
- Never violate the clear space.
- Use the correct positive or reverse artwork.
- A single colour is allowed for sponsorships only, using the proper one-colour artwork.
- Never add graphics or copy to the logo.

## 2. Logo system (p4–p9)

**Primary logo (p4)**
- Colours: PMS 287 + PMS 130.
- Use it whenever possible on light backgrounds, in digital, screen and four-colour print.

**Minimum size (p4)**
- 1" wide in print, 150px wide on screen.
- If that is impossible, make the logo as large as the space allows.

**Clear space (p4)**
- Minimum clear space is 0.5X on all sides, where X is the height of the "N" in the logo.

**Alternate logos (p4)**

| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| Colour-reversed | Primary choice on the darkest brand-colour backgrounds with enough contrast |
| Black | Single- or limited-colour applications |
| White | Secondary- or single-colour applications, or when a lower profile is wanted |

**Monogram (p5)**
- Same PMS 287/130 colours. Clear space is 0.5X, where X is the N height in the monogram.
- Minimum size: .1675" wide in print, 23px wide on screen.
- External use: the full logo or the Newmont name must appear elsewhere in the piece whenever possible.
- Internal use (for example digital signage) may use the monogram alone.
- Reversed, black and white monograms follow the same rules as the logos.

**Misuse (p7). Do not:**
- Scale disproportionately.
- Alter element placement or size.
- Use unapproved colours.
- Rotate "less than 90º" (as printed).
- Apply shadows or effects.
- Recreate the logo in a different typeface.
- Place the logo over busy images or patterns.
- Use backgrounds with insufficient contrast or clashing colours.
- Place the white-boxed primary logo over another background. Use the transparent version.

**BRG marks (p8)**
- Business Resource Group marks are the monogram with the BRG name beneath it (examples: Veterans, Diversitas, Women & Allies, First Nations).
- Each has reverse and single-colour versions.
- Clear space is the same as the monogram. Minimum size .25" or 25px wide.
- An optional colour bar may hold up to six non-brand colours in vertical bands, with no gradients.
- BRG name font and colour: shown, no value given.

**Word marks (p9)**
- CamelCase on one line, for example `NameAccent`.
- First word: Open Sans Bold, -30 tracking, PMS 287.
- Second word: Open Sans ExtraBold, -30 tracking, PMS 2386.
- PMS 287 always comes first. Reverse (white) versions are required for dark backgrounds.
- Never apply word-mark styling to headlines or subheads.
- The legacy pairing PMS 287 + 130 (for example ITGoldBar) predates the accessibility update. Do not use it for new marks.
- Corporate Communications must approve new marks. Unauthorised project or team logos are not permitted.

- Gold #FCAF17 works only with #0C2B82 and #071435. Never put gold text on white, and never white text on gold.

The guide's WCAG notes:
- AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text, and 3:1 for graphics and UI components (WCAG 2.1).
- Large text is 14pt (about 18.66px) bold or larger, or 18pt (about 24px) or larger.
- The matrix key says "bold or larger than 18pt".

## 4. Patterns and gradients (p12–p13)

- Topographic line patterns are a "secondary design element". They come in PMS 287 lines and white (reverse) lines, and serve as page backgrounds or add depth.

**Approved gradients.** There are exactly two, both used under PMS 287 topographic lines:

| Gradient | Top | Bottom |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMS 287 #0C2B82 | PMS 282 #071435 |
| 2 | PMS 2386 #3A68C5 | PMS 287 #0C2B82 |

- Gradient + topo can be a page texture or the background behind text, graphics and logo.
- No other gradients are approved. BRG colour bars, embroidery and foil all say no gradients.

**Reverse (white) topographic pattern**
- Transparent. Use it on PMS 282, 287 or 2386 solids, or on photography.
- It must rise from the bottom of the field: 20–50% opacity at the bottom, fading to 0% at the vertical centre.
- It never extends beyond half the field height.
- On photos, it must not cover the focal point (people, faces).
- Examples show it on a photo with logo and text, and on PMS 2386 with logo and text. A gradient + topo also appears as a band design element and as a full background.
- Pattern line spacing and density: shown, no value given. Use the supplied artwork.

| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| Coloured | White backgrounds |
| Filled (white centre) | Light coloured backgrounds and imagery, or wherever contrast is insufficient |
| White | Dark solid backgrounds and dark imagery |

**Charts (p15)**
- Simple and clear. Artwork must be flat, with no bevels, shadows, gradients or other flashy effects.
- Pie and donut charts need a gap between slices (white separators) for accessibility.
- Include the data within the category area, for example value labels on or next to segments, so there is more than one way to read the data.
- Bar charts may use one colour, or two or more contrasting colours.
- Axes:
  - Rules: .5pt, black.
  - Axis indicators: Open Sans Regular, all caps, 6pt, tracking 100.
- Chart title: Montserrat Bold, 8pt minimum, 50 tracking, all caps (p21). The p15 examples set "CHART TITLE GOES HERE" in Open Sans Bold caps, which conflicts with p21.

## 6. Photography and portraits (p17–p18)

**Executive portraits (p17)**
- Groomed hair and facial hair. Natural makeup (bold lipstick is fine if usually worn).
- Glasses if normally worn, cleaned.
- Solid tops, layered looks (a blazer over a button-up), pressed clothes.
- Small, minimal accessories. Avoid loud or statement pieces and distracting patterns.

**Usage (p18)**
- "Less is more": one powerful image with supporting copy usually suffices.
- When images are related, use at most three per page.
- Approved treatments:
  - Isolate (cut out) subjects in part or in full for depth.
  - A white overlay at 90% opacity to put content over a full-page image.
  - Gradient overlays for contrast.
  - Combine isolated images with patterns and colours.
- Use the p24–p26 grid to size images.
- Images align to the grid with clean, geometric placement. Decorative or non-standard crops are not permitted (p26).
- Subjects shown: site workers in PPE, haul trucks, landscapes.

## 7. Typography (p19–p23)

**Brand typefaces (p19)**
- **Open Sans** for headlines, body copy, subheads, captions and tabular information.
- **Montserrat** for subheads, pull quotes, attributions and tabular information.
- Both are from Google Fonts.
- Open Sans is the primary family and should be used most often. Montserrat adds contrast (p22).

**Approved weights and styles (p19)**
- Open Sans: Regular, Italic, SemiBold, SemiBold Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, Extra Bold.
- Montserrat: Regular, Regular Italic, Bold, Bold Italic.
- Open Sans Light is not in the approved list, but the p30 standard document specifies it. Montserrat SemiBold appears only on PPE (p45).

**System substitutes (p20)**
- Use them only where web or desktop fonts are not supported, such as HTML email and email signatures.
- **Verdana** (preferred) for headlines, pull quotes, captions and tabular information. Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic.
- **Tahoma** (alternate) for body, subheads and tabular information. Regular, Bold.

**Hierarchy (p21).** All sizes are minimums. Tracking is in 1/1000 em.

**Copy colour and small text (p22)**
- Use black or white for copy. Be judicious with brand colours in copy.
- Captions: header Open Sans Bold (Regular or Italic), 6pt min. Copy Open Sans Regular or Italic, 6pt min.
- Address block: header Open Sans Bold 7pt min, lines Open Sans Regular 7pt min.
- Phone and fax:
  - Designate them with a bright-blue capital P and F followed by a space (an en space in design software).
  - Separate numbers with periods.
  - Extensions use "x" with no space before the number.
  - The examples actually use "T" and "F". The bright blue is #0091DA in the p30 sample.
- Web address: no "www.", set in bright blue.

## 8. Document layout and standard documents (p24–p34)

### Grid (p24)

| Format | Columns |
|---|---|
| U.S. letter portrait | 12 |
| U.S. letter landscape | 16 |
| Widescreen 1920x1080 (16:9) | 20 |

- Wider formats use multiples of four. Adjust the column count so the column width stays common across sizes.
- Margins and gutters: shown, no value given.

**Percentages (p25)**
- Always use considerable negative space.
- Divide pages by percentage of page size. 10–50% is acceptable for a colour, copy or image block, and 20%, 30% and 40% give the best contrast.
- Columns take precedence over percentages horizontally. Pick the percentage nearest a column width.
- Images adjoin colour or white. Colour may adjoin white. Be judicious when placing two or more colours side by side.
- Images may sit top, bottom or either side. A page can split colour top and bottom with an image between.
- Examples:
  - A 16:9 slide: image on the left, with a 30% #0C2B82 panel on the right holding headline, text and logo.
  - Portrait: a 40% #071435 top band, then an image, then a 20% white bottom band with the logo.
  - 1080x1080: an image with a 20% #071435 bottom band holding the logo.
  - p26: a 60/40 split.

## 10. Brand application, promotional items, PPE (p42–p45)

- **Embroidery and foil (p42)**
  - Embroidery: monogram or simplified logo, 2" minimum width, satin or fill stitch. No gradients, small text or thin strokes.
  - Foil: 1" minimum width.
  - Corporate Communications approval is required for both.
- **Promotional items (p43)**
  - The logo or monogram must be prominent and legible.
  - If only the monogram is used, the full logo, name or URL appears elsewhere on the item.
  - Co-branding follows p6.
- **PPE (p44–p45)**
  - Hard hats: monogram on the front (1.5" maximum), full logo on the back (3.5" maximum), both .5"–1" above the brim.
  - Safety wear: yellow/navy Bisley only (TT01 hi-vis). Logo centred .5" above the left pocket.
  - Name in Montserrat SemiBold, tracking 100. Department name on the left sleeve.
